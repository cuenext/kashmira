How I can help

Care that meets your child where they are.

Not every child needs the same kind of visit. These are the areas Dr. Kashmira works with most.

01

Anxious & fearful children

Behaviour guidance, trust-building and nitrous oxide conscious sedation when clinically appropriate.

Most requested
02

Prevention & first visits

Fluoride, fissure sealants, SDF, diet guidance and minimally invasive caries care.

03

Special healthcare needs

Individualized care for children with autism, ADHD, cerebral palsy and other additional needs.

04

Fillings, crowns & pulp care

Pediatric restorations, crowns, pulpotomy, pulpectomy and care for developing teeth.

05

Dental trauma & emergencies

Assessment and treatment planning after chipped, displaced, injured or painful teeth.

06

Early orthodontics

Myobrace, Invisalign First assessment, habit correction, space management and growth guidance.

A gentle step-by-step

01Hello first.

We start with the child, their history and what worries them.

02Look, don’t rush.

A calm assessment of teeth, gums, growth, habits and behaviour.

03Explain clearly.

Parents understand the findings, options and why each step matters.

04Treat at their pace.

Prevention or treatment is adapted to cooperation, comfort and need.
