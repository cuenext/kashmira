What I do

From first check-ups to the tricky stuff.

You don’t need to know the dental term before you book. Tell me what’s going on and we’ll figure out the next step.

01

Nervous about the dentist

Gentle behaviour guidance, plenty of patience, and nitrous oxide when it is clinically appropriate.

02

First visits & prevention

Check-ups, fluoride, sealants, diet advice and simple ways to prevent small problems becoming bigger ones.

03

Extra support when needed

Care can be adapted for children with autism, ADHD, cerebral palsy and other additional needs.

04

Fillings, crowns & tooth pain

Restorations, pediatric crowns and pulp treatment when a tooth needs more than a simple filling.

05

Dental injuries & emergencies

For chipped, loose, displaced, injured or suddenly painful teeth.

06

Growing teeth & early orthodontics

Myobrace, Invisalign First assessment, habit correction, space management and growth guidance.

What to expect

A visit doesn’t have to feel complicated.

01Tell me what’s going on.

What hurts, what worries them, and anything you think I should know.

02We take a look.

Teeth, gums, growth and habits — without turning it into a big production.

03I explain what I see.

You’ll know what I found and what the options actually mean.

04We decide what makes sense.

Treatment, prevention or simply keeping an eye on things — depending on the child.
