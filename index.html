Pediatric dentistry, made simpler

Care that grows with your child.

From first visits and prevention to restorative care, pulp therapy and dental trauma, the focus is on practical treatment planning and helping children feel supported throughout their visit.

01

Prevention & growing teeth

Routine check-ups, fluoride, fissure sealants and guidance as baby teeth transition to permanent teeth.

02

Cavities & restorative care

Child-appropriate fillings, pediatric crowns and restorative planning based on each tooth’s needs.

03

Pulp therapy

Pulpotomy and pulpectomy when clinically indicated, with treatment explained clearly to parents and caregivers.

04

Dental trauma

Assessment of chipped, loose, displaced or injured primary and permanent teeth after an accident.

05

Behaviour guidance

Age-appropriate communication and support designed to make dental visits easier for children and families.

06

Conscious sedation

Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may be considered when clinically appropriate following assessment.

What parents can expect

A clear visit from start to finish.

Good pediatric care is not only about the treatment itself. It is also about helping families understand what is happening, what the options are and what comes next.
