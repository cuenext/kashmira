Pediatric care

What we can help with.

Four clear areas of care, with treatment planned only after assessment and discussion with the parent or caregiver.

01

Prevention & growing teeth

Check-ups, fluoride, fissure sealants and guidance as baby teeth transition to permanent teeth.

02

Restorative & pulp care

Fillings, pediatric crowns and pulp therapy such as pulpotomy or pulpectomy when clinically indicated.

03

Dental trauma

Assessment after chipped, loosened, displaced or injured primary and permanent teeth.

04

Anxiety & behaviour support

Age-appropriate communication, behaviour guidance and nitrous oxide conscious sedation when clinically appropriate.

Your child’s visit

Simple, calm, understandable.

Good pediatric care is not about rushing a child through a process. It is about understanding the concern, examining what is needed and explaining the next step clearly.

Plan a visit
1
Meet

We start with symptoms, history, medical information and anything you want us to know.

Findings are explained in plain language and suitable treatment, prevention or follow-up is discussed.
