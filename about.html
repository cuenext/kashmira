Her approach

Good pediatric dentistry starts with trust.

Children experience dental care differently at every age. Dr. Kashmira’s approach combines specialist assessment with age-appropriate communication, helping parents understand the clinical picture while supporting children through each stage of the visit.

Her clinical work includes preventive and restorative pediatric dentistry, pulp therapy, dental trauma care and behaviour guidance. Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may also be considered when appropriate after assessment.

“Clear communication with the child and family is part of the treatment — not an extra.”
