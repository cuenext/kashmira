01 / How I approach care

Getting to know
your child.

Every child has their own worries, preferences and way of communicating.

01

We start by listening.

Tell me what worries your child and what helps them feel comfortable. I use behaviour guidance to support children who feel anxious about dental visits.

02

You’re part of the conversation.

I explain what I find, discuss prevention and treatment options, and make time for your questions.

03

Support for different needs.

I have experience caring for children with additional healthcare and communication needs, including children with autism and ADHD. I adapt each visit to their needs and preferences.
