Why kids?

Children need a different kind of dentist.

Working with children is not just adult dentistry on smaller teeth. You have to understand the child, the parent, their fears, their habits and what stage of growth they are in.

That is why I enjoy pediatric dentistry so much. My work includes prevention, nervous children, special healthcare needs, sedation, pulp treatment, dental injuries and early orthodontic care.
