Dr. Kashmira Pawar Jayprakash is a Specialist Pediatric Dentist with more than nine years of clinical experience. Her work includes prevention, restorative pediatric dentistry, pulp therapy, dental trauma and behaviour guidance.

Care is planned around the child’s age, dental needs, medical information and clinical findings. Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may also be considered when clinically appropriate.
