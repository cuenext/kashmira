A child is more than
their appointment.

Their worries, their personality, their way of communicating — it all matters.

My experience includes behaviour guidance for anxious children and care for children with additional healthcare needs. I focus on clear communication with parents, prevention and treatment planned around the individual child.

From an everyday check-up to more involved care, I want families to understand the findings and the choices available to them.
