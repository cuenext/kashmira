About me

Hi, I’m Kashmira.

I’m a Specialist Pediatric & Preventive Dentist working in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. I’ve spent 11+ years caring for children — especially those who feel nervous, need extra support or simply need a dentist who won’t rush them.

Why kids?

Children need a different kind of dentist.

Working with children is not adult dentistry on smaller teeth. You have to understand the child, the parent, their fears, their habits and what stage of growth they are in.

That’s what drew me to Pediatric & Preventive Dentistry. My work includes prevention, nervous children, special healthcare needs, sedation, pulp treatment, dental injuries and early orthodontic care.
