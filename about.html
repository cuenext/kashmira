About Dr. Kashmira

Specialist care with patience, clarity and a child-first approach.

Dr. Kashmira Pawar Jayprakash is a Specialist Pediatric Dentist practicing in Abu Dhabi, with more than nine years of clinical experience caring for children and adolescents.

Her approach

Good pediatric dentistry starts with trust.

Children experience dental care differently at every age. Dr. Kashmira’s approach combines specialist assessment with age-appropriate communication so that children can feel supported and parents can understand the clinical picture clearly.

Her work includes preventive and restorative pediatric dentistry, pulp therapy, dental trauma and behaviour guidance. Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may also be considered when clinically appropriate after assessment.

Rather than treating every visit the same way, care is planned around the child’s age, dental needs, medical information and clinical findings.
