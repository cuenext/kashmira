## Reference study

The [Dr. Yasmin website](https://www.dryasmin.ae/) was studied for information architecture, parent concerns, doctor personality, first-visit reassurance, cases/testimonials, video/social and interactions. Major pages included [Visit Guide](https://www.dryasmin.ae/dental-referrals/your-visit-guide/), [scared-child care](https://www.dryasmin.ae/pediatric-dental-treatments/dental-treatment-for-scared-children/), [autism care](https://www.dryasmin.ae/dental-care-for-special-needs-children/children-with-autism-spectrum/), [before/after](https://www.dryasmin.ae/before-after/), [Q&A](https://www.dryasmin.ae/pediatric-dentist-q-a/), [Watch Me](https://www.dryasmin.ae/watch-me/) and [Contact](https://www.dryasmin.ae/contact-us/).

Adapted ideas: concern-led entry points, a personal doctor introduction, practical three-step visit journey, accessible FAQ and a clear social connection. The current implementation uses a pink editorial identity, as subsequently requested by the user,, local fonts, portrait compositions, line details, finite entry motion and lightweight native accordions. No reference photography, medical promises, testimonials, copy, color system or page layout was copied. Video and testimonials remain omitted because supplied evidence did not support publishing those areas. Five case galleries were later added from newly supplied folders, with the user explicitly requesting galleries rather than unverified before/after labels. No placeholder sections are displayed.

Full source research and its access limits remain in the task workspace as research-reference.md.

The five later case folders contain 26 photographs and one video. Sixteen close-up photos are published; see asset-provenance.md for source-folder IDs, filenames and export details. Treatment titles follow the supplied folder names, with spelling normalized. No dates, outcomes or treatment-stage claims were added.
