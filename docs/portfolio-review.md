# Native clinical portfolio review · 21 October 2026

The user asked for the entire supplied Canva portfolio to be checked properly and its cases added directly to Cases. The earlier external-link-only implementation is superseded.

All 107 rendered pages were visually inspected. This includes native image comparisons where the browser initially returned progressive previews or stale page captures. The reviewed page decisions and image counts were recorded independently before the production catalog was generated.

- 89 case-bearing source pages form 82 presentation galleries. Eighteen cover, biography, contents and divider pages are excluded from the case library.
- The 82 galleries contain 505 clinical images, radiographs, appliance views and models. The five existing Drive galleries retain their stable IDs and all 16 photographs unchanged: 87 galleries and 521 images overall.
- Eleven treatment categories organize the library. Continuation pages are joined only where the source supports them; multiple examples on a single slide remain separate. A gallery is a presentation group, not a claim that every group is a different patient.
- Two exact repeated images within continuation presentations are retained once. One portrait with no visible mouth and one nonclinical Canva placeholder are excluded. Twelve wider face views use individually reviewed mouth-only crops. Source radiograph headers outside the author's displayed frames are never exported.
- Treatment stages and follow-up intervals appear only when explicitly labelled in the source. Unlabelled views receive neutral descriptions. No ages, diagnoses, outcomes, testimonials or treatment instructions have been invented. The ambiguous material title on page 63 is presented neutrally as “Crown restoration · tooth 21”. The newer CV remains authoritative for biography and qualifications.

The full and thumbnail WebPs preserve clinical pixels, source frame crops, orientation and aspect ratio. Every derivative is reopened and decoded after export.
