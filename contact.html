Before the visit

Quick answers for parents.

When should my child first see a dentist?

A first dental visit is generally recommended by the first birthday, or sooner if there is pain, visible decay, trauma or another concern.

Do baby teeth need treatment?

Yes. Baby teeth support chewing, speech and space for permanent teeth. Treatment depends on the tooth, symptoms and clinical findings.

What if my child is anxious?

Age-appropriate behaviour guidance is part of pediatric care. Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may also be considered when clinically appropriate.

What should I do after a dental injury?

Contact a dental professional promptly if a tooth is chipped, loose, displaced or causing significant pain.
