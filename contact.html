Parent questions

Before the visit.

My child is scared of the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Dr. Kashmira’s work includes advanced behaviour guidance for anxious and uncooperative children, with nitrous oxide conscious sedation considered when clinically appropriate.

Do you see children with special healthcare needs?

Her clinical experience includes children with autism, ADHD, cerebral palsy and other special healthcare needs, with care adapted to the child and family.

Do you provide early orthodontic care?

Her expertise includes interceptive and myofunctional orthodontics, Myobrace, habit correction, space maintenance and Invisalign First assessment.

Which location should I book?

Contact Dr. Kashmira directly and she can guide you to the most suitable current practice location based on availability and your needs.
