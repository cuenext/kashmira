Before your visit

Common parent questions.

When should my child first see a dentist?

A first dental visit is generally recommended by the first birthday, or sooner if there is pain, visible decay, trauma or another concern.

Do baby teeth need treatment?

Yes. Baby teeth support chewing, speech and space for developing permanent teeth. Treatment depends on symptoms, clinical findings and prognosis.

What if my child is anxious about dental treatment?

Age-appropriate communication and behaviour guidance are central to pediatric care. Nitrous oxide conscious sedation may also be considered when clinically appropriate.

What should I do after a dental injury?

Contact a dental professional promptly if a tooth is chipped, loose, displaced or causing significant pain. The right next step depends on the injury and whether the tooth is primary or permanent.
