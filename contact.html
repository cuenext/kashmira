Before you come in

A few quick questions.

My child is scared of the dentist. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Nervous children are a big part of my practice. We can start slowly and adapt the visit to what your child can manage that day.

Do you see children with special healthcare needs?

Yes. My experience includes children with autism, ADHD, cerebral palsy and other additional healthcare needs.

Do you provide nitrous oxide?

Nitrous oxide conscious sedation can be considered when it is clinically appropriate for the child and the planned treatment.

Do you provide early orthodontic care?

Yes. I work with Myobrace, habit correction, space management, growth guidance and Invisalign First assessment.
